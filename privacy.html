Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy covers the game Boxport for iOS and Android, and its free web preview, published by Appnify (AppNify Software) ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the data described here. Contact: appnify.co@gmail.com.

In short

  • There is no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • The game creates an anonymous ID so it can back up your save to the cloud and group crash reports.
  • We use Google Firebase for analytics, crash reports and the cloud save.
  • Rewarded video ads (Google AdMob) play only when you tap to watch one for an in-game reward. No banners, no ads that interrupt play.
  • In-app purchases are paid through the App Store or Google Play. RevenueCat records them so we can deliver and restore what you bought. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

Your anonymous account

The first time the game goes online, Firebase Authentication (Google) creates an anonymous user ID: a random string that is not linked to your name, email, phone number, Apple ID or Google account. It is kept on your device and used for two things: storing your cloud save, and as the user ID on crash reports.

The game also creates a random install id on your device. It is saved with your cloud save so the game can tell whether a cloud copy came from this device, and you can use it to identify your data when you ask us to delete it (see Deleting your data).

There is no password and no way to sign in to the same anonymous account from another device. After you uninstall the game, the connection between you and that account is normally lost.

What we collect and why

Cloud save: Firebase Cloud Firestore (Google)

  • What: your game save (depot, money, staff, story progress and other game state), the in-game day and tier, the platform and OS version (for example "android 14"), the install id and the time of the last save.
  • Why: to keep a backup of your progress and offer to restore it if the copy on your device is older or missing.
  • When: automatically while you play online, at most once a minute.
  • Kept: until you ask us to delete it.

Analytics: Google Analytics for Firebase

  • Game events we send: for example a finished day (day, tier, earnings, stars), a mini-game started or finished (station, score, duration, stars), tutorial steps, story chapters, promotions, new branches, daily challenges, rewarded ads offered and watched, stamps earned and spent, and purchase steps (which product and what it granted, never payment details).
  • Collected automatically by the SDK: an app instance ID, device model, OS and app version, language, approximate country or region (worked out from your IP address), sessions and first launch. On Android it can also read the advertising ID; on iOS only if you allow tracking.
  • Why: to learn which parts of the game are fun, too hard or broken, and to balance the game economy.
  • Kept: event-level data for up to 14 months, then deleted by Google.
  • A switch to turn analytics off is coming to Settings.

Crash reports: Firebase Crashlytics

  • What: when the released game crashes or hits an error: the error and stack trace, device model, OS and app version, free memory and storage, a Crashlytics installation ID, short technical notes from the game (for example "sign-in attempt failed") and your anonymous user ID.
  • Why: to find and fix bugs.
  • Kept: 90 days.

Rewarded ads: Google AdMob (iOS and Android only)

  • When: an ad plays only after you tap a button that offers a reward, such as doubling the day's bonus. The game loads one ad in advance so it is ready.
  • What Google collects: the advertising ID (Android advertising ID; on iOS the IDFA only if you allow tracking), your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads.
  • Why: to show and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, personalise ads.
  • Ad content is limited to family-safe ("G" rated) ads.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: Google's consent form asks for your choice before any ad is requested.
  • iOS: you may see Apple's "Allow tracking?" prompt. If you don't allow tracking (or are never asked), the IDFA is not used.
  • How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In-app purchases: App Store, Google Play and RevenueCat

  • Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never receive your card details, name or billing address.
  • RevenueCat (our purchase service) receives the store receipt (product, price, currency, date, transaction ID, store country), a random RevenueCat user ID created on your device, your device type, OS and app version, and your IP address.
  • Why: to verify purchases, deliver and restore what you bought (for example Boxport Pro), prevent fraud and handle refunds.
  • Kept: as long as needed to provide your purchases and for accounting. Apple and Google keep their own records.
  • RevenueCat's policy: www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Game Center and Google Play Games

If you are signed in to Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android), the game posts your scores to leaderboards and unlocks achievements on that profile. Apple or Google run that profile under their own privacy policies; your gamer name and scores may be visible to other players according to your settings there. We don't receive your Apple ID or Google account.

Stays on your device

  • Game data: your progress, settings (language, sound, controls, reminders), stamp balance, the install id and similar preferences are kept in the app's private storage. Your device's own backup (iCloud, Google) may include them under Apple's or Google's policies.
  • Notifications are local: the game schedules reminders on your device (Koli's check-in, waiting trucks, daily reward). There is no push server and no notification token. The game asks for permission on your second in-game day or when you switch a reminder on. You can turn reminders off in Settings or in your device settings.
  • Sharing: the share button draws a picture of your day report (day, tier, earnings, deliveries, stars, reputation) and opens your device's share sheet. Nothing leaves your device unless you pick an app, and we don't receive it.
  • Store rating: at most once, the game may show Apple's or Google's own rating prompt. We only see what you publish on the store.
  • Motion sensor: if you choose tilt steering, the accelerometer is read live to steer the vehicle. Readings are not stored or sent.

What we don't collect

Name, email address, phone number, postal address, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise location, messages or browsing history. Boxport has no chat and no user-generated content. We don't sell personal data, and apart from the advertising data Google AdMob collects as described above, we don't share it for anyone else's marketing.

Your choices and rights

  • Ads are optional: if you never tap an ad button, no ad is shown (the ad SDK still starts and keeps one ad loaded, as described above).
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in your device settings (Android: Settings › Privacy › Ads; iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking).
  • Ad consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): to change your choice, use the ad privacy options (coming to Settings) or reinstall the game to see the consent form again.
  • Notifications: Settings in the game or on your device.
  • Local data: reset progress in Settings, or uninstall.
  • Your rights: depending on where you live (for example the GDPR in the EU and UK, or KVKK Law No. 6698 in Türkiye), you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to its use, and complain to your data protection authority. Email us; we answer within 30 days.

Deleting your data

  1. In Boxport, open Settings › About and note your install id. (If your version doesn't show it yet, it is coming in an update. Until then, tell us your device model, roughly when you last played and your in-game day and tier, and we will try to find your save.)
  2. Email appnify.co@gmail.com with the subject "Boxport data deletion" and your install id. If you also want your purchase records removed, add the order number from your App Store or Google Play receipt.
  3. Within 30 days we delete your cloud save and anonymous account, and ask RevenueCat to delete the purchase records we can match. Analytics events and crash reports are not stored under your install id; they are deleted automatically after the periods above.

Uninstalling the game removes the data on your device but not the cloud copy.

Children

Boxport is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or the higher age set by law in your country). If you believe a child has used the game, email us and we will delete their cloud save. Ads in Boxport are limited to family-safe content.
